Application: Soulspire Mobile App (Android & iOS)

Soulspire Learning Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Soulspire", "we", "us", or "our") provides the Soulspire mobile application as an educational management and parent-teacher communication portal designed for preschools, teachers, and parents/guardians.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard user information when you use our mobile application and related services. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver educational services, manage student attendance, track developmental milestones, and facilitate school-parent communication:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, registered email address, mobile phone number, and user role (Parent, Guardian, Teacher, or Staff). Accounts are provisioned and authorized through your child's preschool administration.
  • Student Information: Child's full name, assigned class/section, roll number, attendance records, developmental assessments, and teacher comments.
  • User-Generated Content & Media: Photos, documents, homework submissions, and activity records uploaded voluntarily by teachers or parents as part of classroom activities and educational updates.
  • Device & Notification Data: Unique device identifiers and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens required solely for delivering important push notifications (e.g., daily notices, attendance alerts, and announcements).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for authentic educational purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage access to your child's school portal.
  • To record and display daily student attendance, homework, and developmental progress.
  • To distribute school circulars, announcements, and activity photo updates.
  • To send real-time push notifications regarding emergency announcements and school events.
  • To maintain system integrity, troubleshoot bugs, and protect against unauthorized access.

3. Children’s Privacy

Because Soulspire is used in connection with early childhood education, protecting children's privacy is our highest priority:

  • The Soulspire app is operated as an administrative and communication tool for adults (parents, legal guardians, and educators).
  • Student data is collected solely under the direction, consent, and authorization of the participating school and parents/guardians.
  • We do not sell student data.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements or use student information for targeted marketing or commercial behavioral profiling.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. Information is only accessible to authorized school administrators, designated teachers, and the student's verified parents/guardians.

We may share limited data with trusted technical infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Used solely for secure transmission of push notifications to your mobile device.
  • Cloud Hosting & API Infrastructure: Hosted on secured servers with automated backups and encryption.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal and student information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.2+).
  • Local Storage Security: Authentication tokens and sensitive credentials stored on device hardware are secured using encrypted mobile keystores (Flutter Secure Storage).
  • Restricted Access: Access to backend student data is restricted to authorized school personnel through role-based access controls.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal and student data for the duration of the student's enrollment at the institution or as required by applicable educational record-keeping regulations.

Requesting Account or Data Deletion:
Parents and staff can request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time by:

  • Contacting the administrative office of your child's school, or
  • Submitting a formal deletion request via email to support@soulspire.online or visiting our deletion request portal.

Upon verification, personal accounts, authentication tokens, and user-uploaded media files are permanently removed from active systems within 30 days, subject to any statutory record-retention requirements imposed by educational regulatory bodies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
